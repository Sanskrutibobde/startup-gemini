# Product Requirements Document: Milestone Recognition Platform

## 1. Executive Summary
A platform providing physical and digital recognition for micro-creators (1k-50k subscribers) to celebrate milestones, filling the gap left by official platform awards.

## 2. Problem Statement
Micro-creators lack tangible recognition for reaching significant milestones (1k, 10k subscribers). Official platform awards (e.g., YouTube Play Buttons) are reserved for much larger audiences, leaving millions of dedicated creators without a physical symbol of their success.

## 3. Solution
- **Physical Awards:** Custom-manufactured "Bronze" or "Milestone" plaques/buttons.
- **Digital Dashboard:** A community-integrated dashboard tracking growth, milestone progress, and social sharing assets.

## 4. Competitive Landscape
- **Spring/Fourthwall:** Focus on monetization/merch, not milestone recognition.
- **Printful:** Focus on general POD, lacks the 'award' branding and community context.

## 5. Unit Economics
- **ARPU:** $25
- **CAC:** $15
- **Gross Margin:** 60%
- **LTV:** $300
- **LTV:CAC Ratio:** 20:1
- **Payback Period:** 1 month

## 6. Technical Requirements
- Integration with YouTube/Twitch APIs for subscriber verification.
- E-commerce storefront with custom engraving.
- User dashboard for milestone tracking and social media asset generation.
